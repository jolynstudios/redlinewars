						Technical details
						
    
					
    
				
    
				
    
					
    
						
						
    1/9
    
						
					
    
					
    
						
    Controls
    Drag to box-select. Right-click to command; Ctrl + right-click forces fire.
    
						
    Tactics
    Build an ore refinery early: it brings an ore truck, and trucks are your income.
    
						
    Factions
    Russia fields Tesla tanks and shock troopers. Germany’s Chrono Tank teleports.
    
						
    Controls
    Ctrl + 1–6 saves a group. Press the number twice to fly there.
    
						
    Tactics
    Low power slows production. Keep the power bar in the top strip green.
    
						
    Controls
    F, then click: attack-move. Units fight everything on the way.
    
						
    Factions
    France builds decoys, England blinds the enemy, Ukraine drops parabombs.
    
						
    Tactics
    Short game on: lose your base and you lose, even with units left.
    
						
    Controls
    Press M for the full tactical map. The Keys button lists every control.
